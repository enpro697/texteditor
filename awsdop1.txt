(竜胆) 怜空さんの表現は極端だが、技術的には正確だ。ライフサイクルフックはあくまでインスタンスの「状態遷移」をフック（捕捉）して一時停止させるものだ。これを使ってALBのトラフィックルーティングの「パーセンテージ」を制御することはできない。目的が根本的に異なっている。これを正解だと思い込む者は、包丁でネジを回そうとするような愚か者だ。

(Rindo) Reasan's expression is extreme, but technically precise. Lifecycle hooks strictly hook (capture) and pause the "state transition" of an instance. You cannot use this to control the "percentage" of ALB traffic routing. The purpose is fundamentally different. One who believes this to be the correct answer is a fool trying to turn a screw with a kitchen knife.

Rolling is a method where running servers are replaced with the new version sequentially, for example, a few at a time. However, this is a half-dead, half-alive time where old and new versions coexist and are gradually painted over. It is unsuited for precisely controlling traffic to "exactly 10%" and testing for a long period (canary release).
